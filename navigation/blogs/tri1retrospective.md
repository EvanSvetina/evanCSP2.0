        <h2>Trimester 1 Retrospective Questions</h2>
        <h3>1. Personal Growth</h3>
        <p><strong>Q: How do you compare to the beginning of the year?</strong></p>
        <div class="answer">
            I have grown significantly as not just a coder, but also as a group member. Initially I was quite walled off and operated mostly on my own, but as I moved through groups and got to know new people, I became more agile in terms of working with other people. I acted as a leader throughout the trimester and greatly enjoyed organizing and managing work across the classroom. My JS skills have been refined and my Python has significantly grown since the start of the trimester. The first taste of backened development was both scary and hard.
        </div>

        <p><strong>Q: What are your key takeaways from the three sprints (Tools, JS/Python Fundamentals, Digital Famine)?</strong></p>
        <div class="answer">
            Tools: 
            - Nothing new really learned, but helping others get on baord was enjoyable
            JS/Python Fundamentals:
            - Strengthened JavaScript and Python fundamentals through hands‑on exercises and by teaching peers.
            - Managed and scheduled peer‑teaching sessions; organizing and instructing improved communication, leadership, and time management.
            - Built confidence with backend concepts (Flask) and API integration; improved debugging and development workflow.
            - Used playtests to identify UX and gameplay issues and iterated on fixes.
        </div>

        <p><strong>Q: How was your N@tM experience? What memorable feedback did you receive?</strong></p>

        </div>
